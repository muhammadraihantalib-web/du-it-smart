Du-it Smart - Privacy Policy

Du-it Smart is a personal money planner. This page explains what happens to your data.

What we store

The figures you type in (income, commitments, savings, credit card entries) are saved in your own browser. If you choose to sign in with Google, a single file called du-it-smart-ledger.json is saved in your own Google Drive, in a hidden app-data area that only this app can open.

What we can see

The developer has no server and no database. We cannot see, copy or sell your data. The app only asks Google for your email address (to show who is signed in) and for access to its own hidden app-data file in your Drive. It cannot see your other Drive files.

Sharing

Your data is not shared with anyone and no advertising or analytics tools are used.

Deleting your data

Use the Clear buttons in the app, clear your browser data, or remove Du-it Smart from your Google Account permissions at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access lets you delete the app-data file from Drive settings.

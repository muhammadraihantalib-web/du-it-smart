About you

Why fill in the tax status and children?PCB is the income tax taken from your salary every month, and it depends on these answers. Same salary, very different PCB. Example, salary RM 6,000 a month: single about RM 204, married, spouse not working about RM 168, married, spouse not working, 2 children about RM 131. On a lower salary of RM 4,000 the gap is small: single about RM 16, married RM 0. If these are wrong, the PCB is wrong, and so is your take-home. Check your payslip once, and you can always type the real total in the box below.
Put payroll deductions in the EPF + SOCSO + EIS + PCB box, not in the commitments list below, or they are counted twice. Bonuses are worked out from your basic salary.
